Zapwhisker grew up inside the walls of a 24-hour laundromat, living on a strict diet of extension cords. Cheese, she says, is "for rodents with no ambition." Every time she sneezes, three streets lose their Wi-Fi, and the power company has a framed mugshot of her in the break room.

She fights to pay off an electric bill that is, legally speaking, entirely her fault. She is the fastest thing in the tournament and the easiest to knock over: one solid hit sends her spinning like a dropped phone, but good luck landing it.

Her rival is Mudge Bogsworth, who lives in a permanent puddle and still can't work out why his ears go tingly whenever she wanders by.
