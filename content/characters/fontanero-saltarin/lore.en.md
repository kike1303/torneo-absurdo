Handy Hopkins has a mustache with its own union card and exactly one repair technique: if it's broken, jump on it. Pipes, boilers, customers who haggle. He has never once finished a job, but he has jumped on every problem in the county, and a few of them have stopped complaining.

He entered the tournament because someone mentioned "a leak in the arena," and he still believes he's on the clock. He bills for the visit whether he wins or not.

He carries a blowtorch for "soldering," although so far the only thing he's soldered is his own eyebrows. His rival is Spike Hollerman, who still owes him for forty minutes of screaming and one flooded basement.
