Spike has trained since kindergarten on a hill behind a strip mall, and in all those years he mastered exactly two things: hitting really hard and yelling for a really long time. Every tantrum adds another spike to his hair. He now enters rooms sideways and gets his bangs trimmed at a lumberyard.

He's in the tournament because someone promised him "a worthy opponent" and a free sub sandwich afterwards, and nobody, Spike included, knows which one he wants more. His best beam only fires on a full stomach.

His rival is Handy Hopkins, who showed up to fix a leak just as Spike started powering up. Forty minutes of screaming later, the leak was a lake and the handyman was still billing by the hour.
