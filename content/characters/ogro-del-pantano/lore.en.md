Mudge inherited a bog from his grandmother, along with a frog-soup recipe and a lifelong grudge against soap. He's huge, slow and almost impossible to knock down: punches bounce off his belly like pebbles off a pond.

He signed up because first prize includes "a full season with no visitors," and the only thing he hates more than clean water is company. A splash of water scares him more than any punch. He calls it an "allergy." Everyone knows it's the shower.

His rival is Zapwhisker, who moved into his hut and ate his only light bulb. Mudge now reads in the dark, and whenever she scurries past his puddle, his teeth start to fizz.
